# AIIL Service Lifecycle

Version: 1.0 Draft

## Purpose

The AIIL Service Lifecycle defines the stages through which an AI service progresses from initial concept to retirement.

The objective is to ensure AI initiatives are managed consistently, governed appropriately, and continuously improved.

# 1. Strategy

## Purpose

Define why the AI service exists.

## Activities

- Define business objectives

- Identify stakeholders

- Assess risks

- Define success criteria

- Estimate expected value

## Outputs

- Business Case

- AI Service Charter

- Success Metrics

---

# 2. Design

## Purpose

Design the AI service and governance model.

## Activities

- Define requirements

- Select models

- Define controls

- Define ownership

- Define security requirements

## Outputs

- Solution Design

- Governance Plan

- Risk Assessment

---

# 3. Build

## Purpose

Develop and configure the AI capability.

## Activities

- Build agents

- Configure prompts

- Integrate systems

- Create knowledge bases

- Test functionality

## Outputs

- AI Service Package

- Documentation

- Test Results

---

# 4. Deploy

## Purpose

Release the AI service into production.

## Activities

- Approval reviews

- User onboarding

- Production deployment

- Validation

## Outputs

- Production Service

- Release Records

---

# 5. Operate

## Purpose

Manage the AI service in production.

## Activities

- Monitor usage

- Record incidents

- Execute changes

- Measure performance

## Outputs

- KPI Reports

- Incident Records

- Change Records

---

# 6. Improve

## Purpose

Increase business value and maturity.

## Activities

- Review metrics

- Analyze incidents

- Capture lessons learned

- Optimize prompts and workflows

## Outputs

- Improvement Plans

- Updated Metrics

- Optimization Roadmap

---

# 7. Retire

## Purpose

Safely remove AI services that are no longer needed.

## Activities

- Archive documentation

- Remove access

- Retain records

- Communicate closure

## Outputs

- Retirement Report

- Archived Assets

---

# Lifecycle Governance Gates

Gate 1 – Strategy Approval

Gate 2 – Design Approval

Gate 3 – Deployment Approval

Gate 4 – Production Review

Gate 5 – Retirement Approval

---

# AIIL Principle

Every AI Service must have:

- Defined business value

- Assigned ownership

- Risk evaluation

- Ongoing monitoring

- Continuous improvement

- Planned retirement

# Statement

Artificial Intelligence services should be managed as business capabilities, not as isolated technological experiments.

The AIIL Service Lifecycle provides a structured approach for governing, operating, and improving AI services throughout their entire lifecycle.
