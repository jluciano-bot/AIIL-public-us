# AIIL AI Governance Management

Version: 1.0  
Status: Active  
Last Updated: 2026-11-01

---

## Purpose

Ensure that Artificial Intelligence initiatives are aligned with organizational strategy, compliant with policies and regulations, governed by clear accountability structures, and managed within defined risk tolerance.

---

## Scope

All AI initiatives, AI services, AI models, AI agents, and AI-powered workflows across the organization.

---

## Definition

**AI Governance** is the system of policies, processes, roles, accountability structures, and controls that guide how Artificial Intelligence is adopted, managed, and improved within an organization.

---

## Core Governance Principles

1. **Accountability** — Every AI service has a named owner who is accountable for its governance.
2. **Transparency** — AI decisions, capabilities, and limitations are documented and communicated.
3. **Alignment** — AI initiatives are aligned with business strategy and measurable outcomes.
4. **Compliance** — AI services meet applicable regulatory, legal, and ethical requirements.
5. **Risk Awareness** — AI risks are identified, evaluated, and managed within defined tolerance.
6. **Continual Oversight** — Governance is not a one-time exercise; it is sustained through ongoing review.

---

## AI Governance Structure

### Strategic Level

**AI Steering Committee**
- Sets organizational AI direction
- Approves AI strategy and investment
- Reviews AI performance against business objectives
- Escalation path for significant risks

Recommended members:
- Executive Sponsor (CIO, CDO, or equivalent)
- Business leaders from major AI-consuming units
- Legal and Compliance representative
- CISO or Security representative

---

### Operational Level

**AI Governance Board**
- Oversees AI portfolio compliance
- Reviews policy adherence
- Approves governance exceptions
- Monitors AI risk register

Recommended members:
- AI Operations Manager (chair)
- AI Service Owners
- Data Governance Lead
- Security representative

---

### Service Level

**AI Service Owner**
- Accountable for individual AI services
- Ensures compliance with governance policies
- Maintains service documentation
- Reports to AI Governance Board

---

## AI Governance Policy Framework

The AI Governance Policy defines organizational rules for AI adoption and operation. It must address:

### 1. AI Usage Policy
- Approved AI tools and platforms
- Prohibited use cases
- Employee obligations when using AI
- Acceptable use boundaries

### 2. AI Data Policy
- Data classification for AI use
- Sensitive data handling rules
- AI data retention requirements
- Privacy and consent requirements

### 3. AI Model Policy
- Approved model catalog
- Model selection and approval criteria
- Model retirement requirements
- Third-party model risk requirements

### 4. AI Agent Policy
- Agent approval requirements
- Agent ownership obligations
- Agent monitoring requirements
- Agent retirement and decommissioning

### 5. AI Security Policy
- Access controls for AI systems
- Prompt security requirements
- Output validation requirements
- Incident reporting obligations

### 6. AI Ethics Policy
- Bias detection and mitigation requirements
- Fairness and non-discrimination standards
- Human oversight requirements for high-risk AI decisions
- Transparency in AI-generated outputs

---

## Governance Process Flow

### Step 1 — Policy Definition

- Define AI policies aligned with organizational strategy and regulatory requirements
- Review and approval by AI Steering Committee
- Publish to organizational knowledge repository
- Communicate to all stakeholders

### Step 2 — Governance Structure Establishment

- Define and appoint AI Governance roles
- Establish AI Steering Committee and AI Governance Board
- Define escalation paths and decision authority matrix

### Step 3 — AI Portfolio Registration

- Register all AI services in the AI Service Catalog
- Assign ownership for every registered service
- Classify each service by risk level

### Step 4 — Compliance Monitoring

- Periodic compliance reviews against AI policies
- Policy exception management
- Regulatory compliance tracking

### Step 5 — Risk Oversight

- Review AI Risk Register at governance board meetings
- Escalate unacceptable risks to Steering Committee
- Track risk treatment actions to closure

### Step 6 — Governance Review

- Annual review of all governance policies
- Quarterly governance board meetings
- Ad-hoc review triggered by significant incidents or regulatory changes
