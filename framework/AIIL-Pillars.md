# AIIL Core Pillars

Version: 1.0 Draft

The eight pillars are the areas the AIIL Assessment measures. Each pillar has a set of assessment questions.

---

## Pillar 1 - Strategy

Ensure AI initiatives are aligned with business objectives and measurable outcomes.

## Pillar 2 - Governance

Define ownership, accountability, policies, and decision-making structures.

## Pillar 3 - Data

Manage data quality, privacy, classification, and usage policies.

## Pillar 4 - Models

Establish standards for selecting, evaluating, and governing AI models.

## Pillar 5 - Agents

Manage AI agents, automations, responsibilities, and lifecycle.

## Pillar 6 - Operations

Operate AI services using structured, repeatable processes.

## Pillar 7 - Security

Protect AI systems against misuse, data leakage, prompt injection, and compliance violations.

## Pillar 8 - Continual Improvement

Continuously improve AI performance, adoption, governance, and business value.
