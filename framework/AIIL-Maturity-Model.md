# AIIL Maturity Model

## Level 1 - Experimental

AI is used informally.

Characteristics:

- Individual adoption

- No policies

- No governance

- No measurement

---

## Level 2 - Controlled

Basic controls exist.

Characteristics:

- Initial guidelines

- Approved tools

- Limited oversight

---

## Level 3 - Managed

AI processes are documented.

Characteristics:

- Defined ownership

- Standardized procedures

- Training programs

- Performance monitoring

---

## Level 4 - Governed

Governance is established.

Characteristics:

- Formal policies

- Risk management

- Compliance controls

- Auditing capabilities

---

## Level 5 - Optimized

AI is continuously improved.

Characteristics:

- Continuous improvement

- Business-driven optimization

- Advanced automation

- Strategic decision support
