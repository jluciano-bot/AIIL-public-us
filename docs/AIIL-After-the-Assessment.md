# AIIL: After the Assessment

Reviewed by the author on 2026-11-12. The level-by-level focus is a suggestion built from the maturity level descriptions, the Assessment Methodology (Improvement Roadmap Framework) and the Adoption Guide. It is not an AIIL certification and not legal or audit advice.

You finished the AIIL Assessment and have a report. This page tells you what to do with it.

## 1. Read the report in 10 minutes

You do not need to read all six sections. Read these two first:

1. **Executive Summary (section 1).** Your level, your score, your weakest pillars and the top recommendations.
2. **Improvement Roadmap (section 5).** The actions for 90, 180 and 365 days. Each action says when it is done and how much effort it takes.

Use the rest (pillar profile, findings, gap analysis, appendix) when you need the detail behind an action.

## 2. Check that the result is reliable

- The report shows an **evidence basis**: "Evidenced" or "Self-reported". The methodology asks for evidence for every answer above "No". A self-reported result can be too high.
- Review the answers marked "Yes" with the people who know the work. Add an evidence note where you can (a policy, a register, a ticket).
- The result is **confidential**. Share it with your sponsor and the people who took part, not wider.

## 3. Choose two people

- An **executive sponsor**: a senior person who supports the work.
- An **AI governance owner**: the person who drives it. One person plus part-time helpers is enough to start.

## 4. Pick your first actions

Start with the **90-day** list in your roadmap, in the order shown. Do not try to do all 35 questions at once. Aim for three actions you can finish in the first 30 days.

Suggested focus by level (your level is on the first page of the report):

| Your level | Score | Focus first |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Experimental | 0 to 7.5 | List every AI tool in use (the AI Service Register), give each one a named owner, write a short AI policy (two pages at most). |
| 2 Controlled | 8 to 14.5 | Make incident and change handling routine: one way to report an AI incident, one approval step before a change to production AI. Start a risk register. |
| 3 Managed | 15 to 21.5 | Assess the risk of every AI service, classify the data AI may use, add monitoring for the key services, define a few KPIs. |
| 4 Governed | 22 to 28.5 | Close the remaining gaps, complete the security assessments, measure results and keep a fixed improvement cycle. |
| 5 Optimized | 29 to 35 | Keep the level: review on a schedule, test the controls, share what works. |

Level bands are the ones used by the Analyzer (8, 15, 22 and 29 points).

## 5. A simple 90-day rhythm

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1 to 4 | Validate the result with participants and sponsor. Register AI services. Assign owners. Approve a short AI policy. |
| Days 31 to 60 | Put incident and change handling to work. Build the first risk register. |
| Days 61 to 90 | Define KPIs. Hold a lessons-learned session. Run the assessment again. |

## 6. Measure again

Run the assessment again at **about 90 days** and compare with the first one. The Analyzer can compare two results and shows what improved, what got worse and what did not change. After that, reassess every **6 to 12 months**.

Keep the file "Download full result (JSON)" on your own device. Next time, load it with "Load a previous assessment" on the results page to see the progress. You can also load it with "Load a saved copy" on the questions screen to start from your old answers and notes. Use "Save a copy to my device" before leaving a half-finished assessment. The files never leave your browser.

## What this page does not do

It does not replace the Assessment Methodology or the Adoption Guide, and it does not certify anything. The focus per level is a starting point: your own gaps and risks come first.
